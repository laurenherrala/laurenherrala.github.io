Lauren Herrala

Work About Contact

Lauren Herrala is an interdisciplinary artist working primarily in clay. Her sculptural work explores themes of memory and personal history. Incorporating text, sound, and interactive elements, she creates in each piece an invitation to expand our ideas of communication. With the right message carried in the right vessel, one is able to translate a past self, a loved one, a remembered wound. Memories are invoked through poetic text and grounded in material choices.

Handbuilt, slip cast, and thrown clay forms alike are finished by belongings passed down and imbued with memory. A great grandmother's button stash, a sibling’s bedding from childhood, bottles collected by a grandfather. Private, unremarkable objects are recontextualized and recreated to open new channels of communication through time. Materials with personal significance are paired with meditative forms of making. Pattern, repetition, and multiples become ritualistic acts of remembrance. Repetition in the process of making serves to both excavate and selectively alter the remembered past.
